DOORS

A small change to the property images, and a question for you.

Thank you for the six renders. They are beautiful, and the houses in them are exactly right for what DOORS represents.

One thing gave us pause. The landscapes around the houses were not quite the Garden Route. There was lavender and oak where the Outeniquas should be, palm trees on the Pezula ridge, and a headland at Mossel Bay that belonged somewhere warmer and drier. Anyone who lives here would notice, and the whole promise of DOORS is that it knows this coast.

So we have adjusted each image. The houses are untouched. Only the landscape around them has changed, to the plants, rock, water and light that are actually there.

What we would like from you

Simply whether you are happy with the adjusted versions, or would rather we use yours as they were. It is entirely your call, and either is fine with us.

You can also mix them, property by property. Just reply with the ones you want changed and the ones you want left alone.

George Mountain Retreat

What changedThe lavender beds, oak trees and dry golden hills have gone. The range behind the house is now the Outeniquas as they actually stand above George: a long rugged ridgeline, green grass and fynbos on the lower slopes, a dark belt of forest at the foot, big open sky.

What stayedThe house, the water channel, the paving and the planting beds are exactly as you sent them. The sky is clearer than in your version, because that is how the range reads on an ordinary good day here.

Pezula Ridge Residence

What changedThe palm trees have gone and the ridge is planted the way that ridge really is, in fynbos, proteas and restios running down to the sea.

What stayedThe house, the terracing, the pool and your sunset are unchanged.

Mossel Bay Cliff House

What changedThe desert succulents and pale headland are replaced by the darker rocky shoreline and low coastal scrub you actually find along that cliff.

What stayedThe house, the pool, the driveway and the clear blue day are unchanged.

Knysna Headlands Estate

What changedThe gum trees have gone and the slope is now dense indigenous forest falling to sandstone cliffs. The water is the bigger correction: the channel at the Heads is far wider than the first version had it, so the bay now opens out properly with the lagoon and the mountains beyond.

What stayedThe house, the pool, the steps and the lower suite are unchanged.

Wilderness Dune Residence

What changedThe planting is now vegetated dune and dune grass, with the long straight Wilderness beach running away below.

What stayedAlmost everything. This one was already close, so it is the smallest change of the six.

Plettenberg Beachfront

What changedThe arid Mediterranean coastline is now the Plettenberg sweep, with green Tsitsikamma behind it rather than dry brown hills.

What stayedThe house and the terrace are the same, though the framing has opened up a little more than the others. This is the one we would most like your eye on.

One last note

None of this is a criticism of the renders. The likeliest explanation is simply that the brief they were made from never named the landscape, and image tools left to their own devices default to California almost every time.
