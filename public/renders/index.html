The four adjusted property images are now live.

Thank you for the six renders. They are beautiful, and the houses in them are exactly right for what DOORS represents.

On a few of them, though, the landscape around the house was not quite the Garden Route. There was lavender and oak where the Outeniquas should be, palm trees on the Pezula ridge, and a channel at the Knysna Heads far narrower than the real thing. Anyone who lives here would notice, and the whole promise of DOORS is that it knows this coast.

So we adjusted four of the six. The houses are untouched. Only the landscape around them has changed, to the plants, rock, water and light that are actually there. Mossel Bay Cliff House and Plettenberg Beachfront are exactly as you sent them - we looked hard at both and yours are the better images.

Where this stands

All six are now on the site. George Mountain Retreat uses your updated render, the one with the front door closed, with the same landscape adjustment applied to it - so the mountain range you spotted through the doorway is gone.

This page stays up as a record of what changed. If you would rather have any of them back the way you sent it, say the word and we will put it back.

George Mountain Retreat

Your renderThis is your updated one, sent 16 August, with the front door closed. The earlier version we were working from had the range showing through the doorway.

What changedThe lavender beds, oak trees and dry golden hills have gone. The range behind the house is now the Outeniquas as they actually stand above George: a long rugged ridgeline, green grass and fynbos on the lower slopes, a dark belt of forest at the foot, big open sky.

What stayedThe house, the closed front door, the lawn, the water channel, the paving, the retaining wall and the layout of the planting beds are exactly as you sent them. Only the plants filling those beds changed, from lavender to fynbos.

Pezula Ridge Residence

What changedThe palm trees have gone and the ridge is planted the way that ridge really is, in fynbos, proteas and restios running down to the sea.

What stayedThe house, the terracing, the pool and your sunset are unchanged.

Knysna Headlands Estate

What changedThe gum trees have gone and the slope is now dense indigenous forest falling to sandstone cliffs. The water is the bigger correction: the channel at the Heads is far wider than the first version had it, so the bay now opens out properly with the lagoon and the mountains beyond.

What stayedThe house, the pool, the steps and the lower suite are unchanged.

Wilderness Dune Residence

What changedThe planting is now vegetated dune and dune grass, with the long straight Wilderness beach running away below.

What stayedAlmost everything. This one was already close, so it is the smallest change of the six.

One last note

None of this is a criticism of the renders. The likeliest explanation is simply that the brief they were made from never named the landscape, and image tools left to their own devices default to California almost every time.

All six are on the site now, so you can see them in place rather than side by side here.
